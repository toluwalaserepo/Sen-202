About the SIM Project

An academic prototype exploring how technology can improve campus security through community reporting and data visualization.

Introduction

The Security Incident Mapping System (SIM) is a university project designed to help campuses collect, visualize, and respond to security incidents. It brings students, staff, and landlords together on a single platform where reporting is fast, transparent, and easy to understand.

Objectives

  • Provide a simple way to report campus incidents.
  • Map incident locations to identify risk hotspots.
  • Improve response coordination for security teams.
  • Increase transparency and awareness across campus.

Benefits

  • Faster response to emerging incidents.
  • Data-driven decisions for patrol planning.
  • Empowered community with accessible reporting.
  • Better collaboration between roles and stakeholders.

Intended Users

  • Students — report incidents on and around campus.
  • Staff — monitor activity and coordinate responses.
  • Landlords — flag issues affecting student housing.

Why It Matters

Improving campus security is a shared responsibility. SIM demonstrates how a lightweight digital tool can encourage reporting, reduce blind spots, and build a culture of safety — laying the foundation for smarter, safer academic environments.
